Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy sets out the approach taken by Curio Labs (hereinafter referred to as "MyPrints" or "we" or "us" or "our"), an individual proprietorship, toward the data of users and visitors of MyPrints.in (the "Website"). By using our Services, you ("You") agree that your data will be handled in accordance with this Policy. This document is designed to be in coherence with the Information Technology Act, 2000 ("IT Act") and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ( "IT Rules" ).

1. The "Privacy-First" Data Collection

Unlike traditional platforms, MyPrints is designed to function without collecting Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
User Identity: We do not collect your name, email, or phone number. We identify users solely via a randomly generated Universally Unique Identifier (UUID) stored in your browser's cookies.
User Location ( OPTIONAL ): We ask for location data ONLY for purrpose of showing shops nearby.
Order Files: We collect documents/images you voluntarily upload for printing.
Exclusions: This policy does not apply to "Unsolicited Information" you provide through direct emails or social media outside of the standard order process.

2. Our Use of Data

We use your UUID, location (OPTIONAL) and uploaded files strictly to:
- Facilitate printing services between You and your selected print shop.
- To show shop location near you.
- Process payments via our third-party partner, Razorpay.
- Maintain a temporary session to track your order status.

3. Data Storage and The "24-Hour Purge"

Infrastructure: Your files are stored securely on Amazon Web Services (AWS), and our application logic is hosted on PythonAnywhere.
Retention: In accordance with the principle of Data Minimization, all uploaded files are permanently deleted from our AWS servers and the shop's local system 24 hours after upload or immediately after the order is picked up, whichever occurs first.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We engage authorized service providers to perform specific functions:
Payment Gateway: Financial transactions are handled by Razorpay. We do not store your credit card or bank details.

5. Cookies and Your Rights

Functional Cookies: We use a persistent cookie to store your UUID so you can access your order.
- User Choice: You can visit the Website without cookies, but you will not be able to use the printing features.
- Right to Erasure: You may dissociate your device from MyPrints at any time by clearing your browser cookies. This act instantly deletes your UUID and terminates your access to any pending order data.

6. Security

MyPrints takes reasonable steps to protect your data from loss or unauthorized access. We use secure AWS environments and encrypted transit. However, please remember that no Internet transmission is 100% secure; we encourage caution when uploading sensitive documents.

7. Children's Privacy

MyPrints is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children without verifiable parental consent.

8. Governance and Dispute Resolution

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising out of or in relation to this Policy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Jalna, Maharashtra, India.
