The Process

5 steps.
Under 60 seconds.

From hostel to holding your prints — faster than finding a pen in your bag.

1
🏪
Select Your Shop
Pick the nearest open xerox shop on campus from the list.
2
📄
Upload & Configure
Upload your PDF, choose B&W or colour, single or double-sided, number of copies.
3
🛒
Review & Pay
Check your order summary, then pay directly to the shop via GPay or PhonePe. We never touch your money.
4
🔑
Get Your OTP
A unique 6-digit code is generated for your order. Screenshot it — you'll need it at the counter.
5
📦
Walk In & Collect
Show the OTP at the counter. Your prints are already waiting. No queue, no wait.
